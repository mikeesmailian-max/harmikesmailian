About

Freight is a promise. My job is keeping it.

Every load is somebody's deadline. That's the whole job, and it hasn't changed since 1999.

I lead Mega Fleet Logistics, a for-hire interstate carrier headquartered in Pasadena, California. We haul dry van and reefer freight — one-way, dedicated, local, and expedited — for shippers who need their loads to arrive on time and undamaged, the first time.

The business was built on a simple premise: modern equipment, professional drivers, and enough investment in technology that a customer never has to wonder where their freight is. Three decades of industry experience sit behind every lane we run.

I spend most of my time on the parts of this business that don't scale by themselves — capacity planning, customer relationships, and making sure the operation stays as accountable at 1,200 customers as it was at ten.
